# 🔬 Research Methodology for Graduate Computer Engineering Students
# روش‌شناسی پژوهش (مهندسی برق و کامپیوتر)

A graduate-level course on research methodology tailored specifically for **Computer Engineering** students. This course guides students through the full research lifecycle — from formulating a research problem to writing and presenting a research report — with an emphasis on **algorithmic**, **systems**, **software engineering**, and **AI/ML** research contexts.

 درس روش‌شناسی پژوهش برای دانشجویان تحصیلات تکمیلی مهندسی برق و کامپیوتر، با تمرکز بر پژوهش‌های الگوریتمی، سیستم‌های دیجیتال و کامپیوتری، شبکه هوشمند و مهندسی نرم‌افزار و هوش مصنوعی/یادگیری ماشین.

## 🎯 Course Objectives | هدف کلی

This course prepares graduate Computer Engineering students to conduct **rigorous, reproducible, and ethically sound research**. Students will learn how to:

- Formulate a researchable problem in computer engineering domains
- Conduct systematic literature reviews (SLR/MLR)
- Design experiments, benchmarks, and system evaluations
- Collect and process quantitative and qualitative data
- Write research proposals, theses, and papers in IEEE/ACM format
- Evaluate research artifacts and publish reproducible results

هدف این درس، آماده‌سازی دانشجویان برای انجام پژوهش‌های دقیق، تکرارپذیر و اخلاقی در حوزه‌های مهندسی کامپیوتر است.

## 📚 Course Syllabus | سرفصل‌ها

### 1. Research: A Way of Thinking | پژوهش: یک شیوه تفکر
- Research as an integral part of engineering practice
- Research as a way to gather evidence for design decisions
- Applications of research in computer engineering
- What does research mean? Definitions and misconceptions
- The research process: characteristics and requirements
- Types of research:
  - From **application perspective**: pure vs. applied research (e.g., theoretical CS vs. applied ML)
  - From **objectives perspective**: descriptive, correlational, explanatory, exploratory
  - From **mode of enquiry perspective**: structured vs. unstructured (quantitative vs. qualitative)
- Paradigms of research (positivism, interpretivism, design science)
- **Computer Engineering focus**: design science research, empirical software engineering, systems research, ML/AI research paradigms

### 2. The Research Process: A Quick Glance | نگاهی کلی به فرایند پژوهش
- The research process: an **eight-step model**
- **Phase I**: Deciding what to research
  - Step I: Formulating a research problem
- **Phase II**: Planning a research study
  - Step II: Conceptualising a research design
  - Step III: Constructing an instrument for data collection
  - Step IV: Selecting a sample
  - Step V: Writing a research proposal
- **Phase III**: Conducting a research study
  - Step VI: Collecting data
  - Step VII: Processing and displaying data
  - Step VIII: Writing a research report
- **Computer Engineering focus**: applying the eight-step model to algorithm design, system building, and ML experimentation

### STEP I — FORMULATING A RESEARCH PROBLEM | تدوین مسئله پژوهش

#### 3. Reviewing the Literature | مرور ادبیات
- The place of the literature review in research
- Bringing clarity and focus to your research problem
- Improving your research methodology
- Broadening your knowledge base
- Enabling you to contextualise your findings
- How to review the literature:
  - Searching for existing literature (IEEE Xplore, ACM DL, Scopus, arXiv, Google Scholar)
  - Reviewing selected literature
  - Developing a theoretical framework
  - Developing a conceptual framework
  - Writing about the literature reviewed
- **Computer Engineering focus**: Systematic Literature Reviews (SLR), Mapping Studies, PRISMA guidelines, reproducible literature search strategies, managing references with BibTeX/Zotero

#### 4. Formulating a Research Problem | تدوین مسئله پژوهش
- The research problem
- Importance of formulating a research problem
- Sources of research problems (gaps in literature, industry needs, benchmark limitations)
- Considerations in selecting a research problem
- Steps in formulating a research problem
- Formulation of research objectives
- The study population (datasets, benchmarks, systems)
- Establishing operational definitions
- Formulating a research problem in qualitative research
- **Computer Engineering focus**: problem statements for algorithmic, systems, and ML research; defining scope, assumptions, and evaluation criteria

#### 5. Identifying Variables | شناسایی متغیرها
- What is a variable?
- Difference between a concept and a variable
- Converting concepts into variables
- Types of variables:
  - From the viewpoint of causal relationship (independent, dependent, extraneous, confounding)
  - From the viewpoint of study design (active, attribute)
  - From the viewpoint of unit of measurement
- Types of measurement scale:
  - Nominal or classificatory scale
  - Ordinal or ranking scale
  - Interval scale
  - Ratio scale
- **Computer Engineering focus**: performance metrics (runtime, accuracy, F1, latency, throughput, energy), workload characteristics, hardware/software parameters, dataset properties

#### 6. Constructing Hypotheses | ساخت فرضیه‌ها
- Definition of a hypothesis
- Functions of a hypothesis
- Testing of a hypothesis
- Characteristics of a hypothesis
- Types of hypothesis (null, alternative, directional, non-directional)
- Errors in testing a hypothesis (Type I, Type II)
- Hypotheses in qualitative research
- **Computer Engineering focus**: hypotheses in algorithm analysis ("Algorithm A outperforms B on graph class C"), ML experiments ("Model M generalises better than baseline"), and systems research ("Optimisation X reduces latency by Y%")

### STEP II — CONCEPTUALISING A RESEARCH DESIGN | طراحی پژوهش

#### 7. The Research Design | طراحی پژوهش
- What is a research design?
- Functions of a research design
- The theory of causality and the research design
- **Computer Engineering focus**: design science, experimental design, quasi-experimental design for software engineering, simulation-based design

#### 8. Selecting a Study Design | انتخاب طرح مطالعه
- Differences between quantitative and qualitative study designs
- Study designs in quantitative research:
  - Based on number of contacts (cross-sectional, longitudinal)
  - Based on reference period (retrospective, prospective)
  - Based on nature of investigation (experimental, non-experimental, quasi-experimental)
  - Other designs: cross-over, replicated cross-sectional, trend, cohort, panel, blind, double-blind
- Study designs in qualitative research:
  - Case study
  - Focus groups / group interviews
  - Participant observation
  - Holistic research
  - Reflective journal log
- Other philosophy-guided designs:
  - Action research
  - Participatory and collaborative research
- **Computer Engineering focus**: 
  - **Controlled experiments** in software engineering
  - **Benchmarking** and **ablation studies** in ML/AI
  - **Case studies** in industry settings
  - **Simulation-based evaluation** for networks and distributed systems
  - **User studies** in HCI
  - **Reproducibility studies** and artifact evaluation

  - Functions of attitudinal scales

  - Types: Likert, Thurstone, Guttman

  - **Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)** surveys

#### 11. Establishing Validity and Reliability | اعتبار و پایایی ابزار پژوهش

- **Computer Engineering focus**:

  - **Construct validity** of performance metrics
